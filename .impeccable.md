## Design Context

### Users
Researchers, students, academic evaluators, supervisors, and technically literate reviewers exploring evidence fusion workflows. They need to quickly understand what Fusion Lab can demonstrate, why fusion is useful under uncertainty, and how to try the core flows without reading documentation first.

### Brand Personality
Credible, precise, modern. The interface should evoke trust, clarity, and intellectual confidence rather than startup hype.

### Aesthetic Direction
Scientific editorial: strong typography, structured storytelling, restrained but high-quality visual accents, clear diagrams instead of decorative fluff. Keep the current app shell, but make the home view feel intentionally curated and presentation-ready.

### Design Principles
1. Explain value before showing controls.
2. Prefer visual structure over card clutter.
3. Make entry points obvious within one screenful.
4. Keep copy concise, specific, and in English.
5. Preserve academic credibility in both light and dark mode.
